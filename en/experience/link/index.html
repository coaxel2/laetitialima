The role

Digital project manager at Link agency in Bordeaux. The role is to bridge clients and internal teams, from the initial request through to final delivery, ensuring deadlines are met and deliverables are of quality.

Responsibilities

Digital project management

Taking on, coordinating and tracking digital projects, from the initial request through to final delivery.

Client relations

Supporting clients throughout their projects, following up on requests and maintaining regular communication to ensure their satisfaction.

Briefing

Analysing client needs, clarifying objectives and formalising the information required to deliver projects properly.

Team coordination

Acting as the link between clients and internal teams, passing on briefs, centralising feedback and tracking each stage of production.

Schedule management

Creating, updating and tracking project schedules in the dedicated software, to keep deadlines and priorities on track.

Operational follow-up

Monitoring progress, anticipating blockers and following up with the various stakeholders.
