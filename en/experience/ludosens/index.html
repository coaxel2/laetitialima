The company

Ludosens is an association committed to social and professional inclusion, designing and running educational and creative projects around neurodiversity, mental health and young people's agency, through innovative approaches combining play, awareness and support.

My responsibilities

  • Producing visuals, videos and copy tailored to each communication channel.
  • Designing and rolling out editorial plans to strengthen visibility (+80%) and engagement (+50%).
  • Coordinating internal and external communication across several channels (social media, newsletters, print materials).
  • Helping design and roll out the communication strategy to showcase the association's projects and activities.
  • Coordinating, tracking and developing activities and projects.
  • Analysing performance on Meta Business and adjusting strategies to maximise online impact.

Key highlights

Designing and leading projects for the programmes

Designing, structuring and leading projects around the youth programmes (Potentiels en mouvement and Valorisons toutes les formes d'intelligence).

Creating communication materials

Producing and modernising materials for teams, partners and young people: programme presentations, flyers, summary documents and materials to help explain the work carried out.

Building the communication strategy

Designing and implementing the association's entire communication strategy, including the editorial line, schedule, channel selection and message consistency across every medium.

What I learned

This role at Ludosens was particularly formative, as it helped me understand what inclusive, accessible communication really involves. I learned to design content and materials that account for the sensitivities, pace and specific needs of the audiences supported around neurodiversity and mental health — something that profoundly changed the way I think about communication and marketing.

Working within a multidisciplinary team also taught me to collaborate with different professional profiles, adapt to constraints and turn high-impact social projects into clear, engaging and consistent messaging, while respecting the organisation's values and objectives.
