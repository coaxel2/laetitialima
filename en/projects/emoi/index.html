The project

EMOI is the design of an innovative interactive kiosk allowing anyone to create their own bespoke fragrance from neuroscientific and sensory cues. Conceived as an immersive experience, the project combines technology, emotion and personalisation to offer a unique user journey, where scent choices are guided by the user's emotions and feelings. The project was selected to represent the campus at the national Ydays competition.

The concept

EMOI rests on the idea that a fragrance should not be chosen, but revealed. The project draws on a neuroscientific approach to turn what is often an intuitive decision into an immersive experience. Through two distinct entry points, users are invited to explore their emotions or their sensory preferences to arrive at a creation that genuinely reflects them.
